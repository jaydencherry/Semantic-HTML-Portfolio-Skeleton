About Me

I chose the Games & Interactive Media major at Michigan State University because it was the only school that had a bachelors program dedicated to video games.

Game design interests me the most, specifically game mechanics. The mechanics can make or break a game and some of my biggest inspirations are Hollow Knight and Deltarune.

After graduating I'd like to work at a game company under a game design role. If that path doesn't align for me I'd also like to branch into software engineering.

Projects

AboveTale

AboveTale is a solo 2D RPG project I created in Unity. The game features an explorable overworld, interactive NPC dialogue, a custom bullet-hell combat system inspired by Undertale, and original music that I composed and added to the game.

  • NPC dialogue system
  • Bullet-hell combat
  • Original soundtrack
  • C# scripting

Play AboveTale on itch.io

Zap Attack!

Zap Attack! is a solo arcade-style space shooter I created in Unity. It includes multiple levels, increasing difficulty, an infinite enemy spawner stage, asteroid obstacles, and original music made in FL Studio.

  • Progressive difficulty
  • Infinite enemy spawning
  • Asteroid obstacle mechanics
  • Original FL Studio music

Play Zap Attack! on itch.io

Arcane Adrenaline

Arcane Adrenaline is a collaborative tabletop game where players control wizards racing through the Dungeon of Doom using three-card spell combos. The project includes a custom elemental Spell Deck and a Dungeon Deck containing traps, bosses, and branching paths.

I worked with Aiden Bunnell and Quinton Mazzara to design and illustrate more than 70 unique cards while also helping with game balancing and the overall gameplay systems.

  • 70+ cards designed and illustrated
  • Four elemental card types
  • Boss and dungeon mechanics
  • Game balancing
